## Academic writing

For any research paper, thesis, report, abstract, LaTeX manuscript, or writing review, use the academic-writer-humanizer skill at `<SKILL_DIR>`.
Start by reading `<SKILL_DIR>/SKILL.md` and follow its section 3 (read the whole project, build PROJECT_BRIEF.md and VOICE_PROFILE.md) before writing. Use `templates/paper_template.tex` for new LaTeX manuscripts.

Key manuscript standards:
- 6-section standard: Max 6 main sections (Intro, Conventional Method / Corpus, Proposed Method, Experiments, Results, Conclusion).
- Subsections: 4–6 core thematic groupings per section max; strictly avoid 5.1–5.10 sprawl.
- Author voice: Calibrated default baseline (sentence length StDev ~10.8 words, substantive 150–250+ word paragraphs, no 3–4 line micro-paragraphs).
- Equations: Follow display equations with lowercase, unindented `where $x$ denotes...` (never `here a = ...`).
- Figures: 600+ DPI, legible text (>=8 pt), concise captions (<=35 words; place analysis in prose).
- Experiments: Include formal Evaluation Metrics subsection with mathematical formulas.
- Conclusion: Exactly one cohesive single paragraph (120–160 words).
- Zero AI clichés: No tapestry, delve, crucial role, furthermore, or in conclusion.
- Research integrity: Never invent results, numbers, or citations. Ground all claims in evidence.
