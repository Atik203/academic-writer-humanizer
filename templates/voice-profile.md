| Feature | Calibrated Default Author Style |
|---|---|
| **Person and voice** | Balanced academic voice: `"We"` for methodological and experimental decisions (`"We evaluated..."`); passive voice for mathematical operations and data processing (`"Trees are added..."`, `"Rows are mapped..."`). |
| **Tense habits** | **Past tense** for procedures, experiments, and observed numbers; **Present tense** for mathematical formulations, algorithms, and table/figure narration. |
| **Sentence rhythm** | **High Burstiness**: Mean sentence length = 20.4 words, StDev = 10.8 words (variance spans 3 to 73 words). Combines short punchy statements with multi-clause compound sentences. |
| **Paragraph shape** | **Substantial, unified paragraphs**: Mean = 225.5 words, Median = 207 words. Strictly zero 3–6 line micro-paragraphs. |
| **Connectors & transitions** | Subordinating conjunctions: `While`, `Although`, `Since`, `Because`. Restrained logical adverbs: `Therefore`, `However`, `Thus`. **Strict ban on AI clichés**: zero `Furthermore`, zero `Moreover`, zero `In conclusion`. |
| **Hedging style** | Direct and empirically bounded (`"on the evaluated datasets"`, `"remains near chance"`, `"operates as a domain-specific screening score"`). No vague softening (`"somewhat"`, `"fairly"`). |
| **Equations & variables** | Display equations followed immediately by lowercase `where $a$ denotes...`, defining every variable and dimension. **Never use informal syntax** like `"here a = ..."`. |
| **Section Architecture** | Exactly **6 main sections standard** (Intro, Conventional, Proposed, Experiments, Results, Conclusion). Core subsections only (no 4.1–4.8 sprawl). |
| **Conclusion style** | Strictly **ONE single cohesive paragraph** (~120–160 words). No bullets, no multiple paragraphs. |
| **Visuals & captions** | 600+ DPI minimum, large legible fonts on axes/legends, concise 1–2 line captions with in-depth analysis in the body text. |

**Keep:** High sentence length variance, substantial single-topic paragraphs, formal `where...` variable declarations, and evidence-grounded claims.
**Never copy:** AI filler transitions (`Furthermore`, `Moreover`), fragmented micro-paragraphs, or informal math notations.
